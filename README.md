# Sephiroth Parameter Rework

A gameplay parameter overhaul for **Sephiroth** in *Super Smash Bros. Ultimate*, focused on refining his responsiveness, hitbox reach, and overall balance.

## Overview

This mod reworks several of Sephiroth's gameplay parameters to create a more responsive and refined experience while preserving his identity as a powerful, long-reaching swordfighter.

The changes focus on three primary areas:

- **Reduced End Lag:** Decreases the end lag on many of Sephiroth's moves, allowing for smoother transitions between attacks and improving his overall responsiveness.
- **Masamune Disjoint Adjustments:** Modifies the disjoint of Sephiroth's Masamune to refine the effective reach of his attacks and improve hitbox interactions.
- **Weight Adjustment:** Changes Sephiroth's weight to alter his survivability and overall character balance.

## Gameplay Changes

| Category | Modification |
|---|---|
| End Lag | Reduced on multiple moves |
| Masamune | Adjusted disjoint |

## Goals

The primary goals of this rework are to:

- Improve Sephiroth's responsiveness and overall fluidity.
- Refine the interaction between his sword hitboxes and opponents.
- Adjust his weight to achieve a different balance between survivability and character strengths.
- Preserve his unique playstyle and identity.
